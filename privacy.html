Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Creativivid Studio processes personal data submitted through consultation and data-rights requests on this website.

1. Purpose of Data Processing

We process submitted information to review inquiries, respond to requests, evaluate potential collaboration, and handle data-rights requests.

2. Data We Process

  • Contact details you provide (for example, name and email address)
  • Restaurant/brand and optional location or project context details
  • Menu situation, collaboration level, timeline, and optional message fields
  • Data-rights request details submitted through the privacy request form

3. Legal Basis

Consultation data is processed based on consent. Data-rights requests are processed to comply with legal obligations under applicable data protection law.

4. Retention

We keep data only as long as necessary for request handling, follow-up, and legal compliance. If no collaboration proceeds, data is deleted within a reasonable period unless retention obligations apply.

5. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection to processing where applicable. You may also withdraw consent at any time.

We aim to respond without undue delay and normally within one month, as required by applicable law.

6. Third-Party Processor and Transfers

Consultation and privacy requests may be processed through Formspree (form backend service) acting as a processor.

Where data is transferred outside the EEA, appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) are applied according to provider documentation.

Cloudflare Turnstile may process technical interaction data to distinguish human visitors from automated abuse and protect forms against spam.
