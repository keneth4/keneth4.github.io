Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Creativivid Studio processes personal data submitted through the consultation form on this website.

1. Purpose of Data Processing

We collect contact and project information to review your inquiry, respond to your request, and discuss possible collaboration.

2. Data We Process

  • Name and email address
  • Restaurant/brand and location details
  • Budget/timeline context and project message

3. Legal Basis

Data is processed based on your explicit consent when submitting the consultation form and requesting contact.

4. Retention

We keep inquiry data only as long as needed for consultation and follow-up. If no collaboration proceeds, data is deleted within a reasonable period unless legal retention obligations apply.

5. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal data by contacting k.ent@hotmail.com.

6. Third-Party Processor

Consultation form submissions may be routed through Formspree (form backend service). Their processing is governed by their own privacy and security policies.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, email k.ent@hotmail.com.
